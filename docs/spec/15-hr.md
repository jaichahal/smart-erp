# 15 HR

Phase 11 is the employee record for one UAE trading company, one legal entity in v1. Every row still carries `company_id` (R1.1). Payroll is Phase 12, specified separately in `16-payroll.md`. Phase 12 depends on Phase 11. Phase 12 reads the employee fields in this file and does not invent a second employee. Phase 11 stores those fields. It does not calculate pay, allowances, deductions, or end of service.

Expense claims and staff advances stay on P7.3 (R10.2, acceptance C29 and C30). Phase 11 references them. It does not move them.

This phase has no recruitment website, no performance-review product, and no learning management.

Phase 11 is test-driven when it is built. Tests for HR1 onward are written first, and seen failing, before implementation. This file does not implement the phase. Acceptance cases are listed here. Task lines in `07-tracks-and-tasks.md` are unchanged.

Format: ID, title, track, depends on, requirements, acceptance. Same shape as `07`.

## Employee fields Phase 12 must consume

Phase 12 consumes the current approved values below. Grade, cost centre, and wage bank account are effective-dated versions (R3.8). Phase 12 selects the version in force on a date. Phase 11 does not turn any of these into an amount.

- `id`, `company_id`, `name`.
- `user_id`, nullable. Set when this person already has a user from P1.3. Empty when they have no login.
- ERP roles, read from that user when `user_id` is set. The employee row does not keep a second copy and does not grant roles. Role changes stay on P1.3.
- `department_id`.
- `manager_id`, nullable employee id. Empty only when this employee has no manager.
- `cost_centre_id`, an existing cost centre dimension (R4.3).
- `start_date`.
- `nationality`, required country.
- `nationality_class`, required: `uae_national` or `expatriate`. UAE payroll differs for nationals and expatriates. Phase 11 stores the class. Phase 12 applies it.
- `employment_type`: `full_time`, `part_time`, or `fixed_term`.
- `status`: `active` or `disabled`. `disabled_at` is the timestamp of the disable transition.
- `grade_code` on the approved grade version (`effective_from`, `effective_to`). A code, not an amount.
- Wage bank account on the approved version (`effective_from`, `effective_to`): `account_holder_name`, `bank_name`, `iban`.
- Identity expiry dates Phase 12 may read: `passport_expiry`, `visa_expiry`, `emirates_id_expiry`, `labour_card_expiry`. Each is nullable until recorded. Reminders are this phase. Pay effects are Phase 12.

Leave is a separate document Phase 12 may read: `employee_id`, `leave_type` (`annual`, `sick`, `unpaid`), `start_date`, `end_date`, `working_days`, `status`. The pay effect of leave is Phase 12.

## Requirements

IDs start at R20.1 so they do not collide with R1 through R18 in `01-requirements.md`.

- R20.1 An employee master SHALL hold name, an optional link to an existing user, department, cost centre, start date, nationality, employment type, and status. The link SHALL show that user's existing ERP roles and SHALL NOT grant roles. At most one employee in the company SHALL link to a given user. Cost centre SHALL be an existing cost centre dimension. Employees SHALL be disabled, never deleted, and a disabled employee SHALL remain resolvable.
- R20.2 Nationality SHALL be required. `nationality_class` SHALL be `uae_national` when the country is the United Arab Emirates and `expatriate` otherwise. A missing country or class SHALL be refused. Phase 11 SHALL store the class because UAE payroll differs for nationals and expatriates, and SHALL NOT compute pay from it.
- R20.3 Passport, visa, Emirates ID, and labour card SHALL be recorded as expiry dates with alerts from the existing alert rules (P1.8, R13.7). An optional copy SHALL use the existing write-once attachment store (P1.9, R3.10). The product SHALL record dates the company already holds. It SHALL NOT issue, alter, or fabricate those documents.
- R20.4 A leave request SHALL be approved by the existing approval engine (P1.7, R2). Phase 11 SHALL NOT add a second engine or a second state machine. Working days SHALL use the Phase 1 holiday calendar (P1.4, R13.8): a date marked weekend or holiday is not a leave day.
- R20.5 Org placement SHALL be the employee's department and one manager, another employee of the same company, or none at the top. An employee SHALL NOT be their own manager. This phase SHALL NOT include a recruitment website, a performance-review product, or learning management.
- R20.6 Changes to salary-relevant fields SHALL be audited with before and after (R3.12) and SHALL pass four-eyes on the existing approval engine before they take effect. The salary-relevant fields are grade, cost centre, and the bank account for wages. Each change SHALL be an effective-dated version. Phase 11 SHALL list and protect these fields and SHALL NOT design the payroll calculation.
- R20.7 A wage bank-account change SHALL follow vendor bank-account change (R8.1, R1.6, P2.2, P6.1): an approval document, the user who entered it cannot approve it, step-up, an exceptions-report row, and the previous version kept for anything that already referenced it.
- R20.8 HR documents SHALL NOT be visible to sales roles. HR documents here are identity expiry rows and their copies, another employee's leave, grade, and the wage bank account. An Accountant SHALL see the employee's cost-centre assignment and SHALL NOT see grade, wage bank account, or identity document detail in this phase. Payroll amounts, payslips, and the permissions on them wait for Phase 12. Counts SHALL use the same filter as the rows (R1.11).
- R20.9 Expense claims and staff advances SHALL remain P7.3. Phase 11 SHALL NOT register a second claim or advance type. A P7.3 claim or advance MAY name an employee id from this master. Posting and clearing stay C29 and C30.
- R20.10 Phase 12 SHALL depend on Phase 11 and SHALL consume the employee fields listed in this file. Phase 12 SHALL NOT redefine that master.

## Acceptance

One-line cases, same shape as `08`. Written as failing tests when Phase 11 is built. Not implemented now.

- HR1 An employee saved without nationality is refused, and the error names the field.
- HR2 `nationality_class` is `uae_national` only for the United Arab Emirates and `expatriate` for any other country; a blank class is refused.
- HR3 An employee linked to a user shows that user's P1.3 roles, and the link grants no role.
- HR4 An employee with no `user_id` has no ERP role and cannot authenticate as that employee.
- HR5 A second employee linked to the same user in the company is refused.
- HR6 Disabling an employee keeps the row; delete is refused; the disabled employee stays resolvable.
- HR7 Cost centre must be an existing cost centre dimension; any other dimension is refused.
- HR8 Passport, visa, Emirates ID, and labour card expiry dates raise reminders through the existing alert rules before the date.
- HR9 The identity reminder has no operation that issues or rewrites a passport, visa, Emirates ID, or labour card.
- HR10 An expiry reminder still fires when no attachment is stored.
- HR11 A leave request is submitted to the Phase 1 approval engine, and the submitter cannot approve it.
- HR12 Leave working days exclude dates the Phase 1 holiday calendar marks as weekend or holiday.
- HR13 Leave status changes only through the approval engine; a direct status write is refused.
- HR14 A leave rejection with an empty reason is refused.
- HR15 Department is stored on the employee, and a manager, when set, is another employee of the same company.
- HR16 An employee set as their own manager is refused.
- HR17 Recruitment postings, performance reviews, and learning enrolments are not operations of this phase.
- HR18 A change to grade, cost centre, or wage bank account writes an audit event with before and after and stays inactive until a different user approves it.
- HR19 The user who enters a wage bank-account change cannot approve it; step-up is required; the change is on the exceptions report; the stored account has holder, bank name, and IBAN.
- HR20 A registered wage bank change adds an effective-dated version and leaves the previous version in place for existing references.
- HR21 An in-place edit of grade, cost centre, or wage bank account that skips the approval document is refused.
- HR22 A Sales Agent receives no identity document, no other employee's leave, no grade, and no wage bank account, and the list counts match that filter.
- HR23 An Accountant reading an employee receives the cost-centre assignment and does not receive grade, wage bank account, or identity document detail.
- HR24 No Phase 11 response includes a pay amount, a payslip, or a payroll permission.
- HR25 Expense claims and staff advances remain the P7.3 documents; this phase adds no second type; a claim may store this employee id.
- HR26 The console employee screen shows cost centre to an Accountant, hides grade and wage bank from that role, and submits leave into the shared approval queue.

## Test-driven development

When Phase 11 is built, each behaviour starts as a failing test named by its HR id. Backend cases are Go tests against the real handler and Postgres before the handler behaviour. The console case is a Vitest or Playwright test before the screen behaviour, per the test-driven rule in `10-execution-playbook.md`. The order is the failing test, the failure for the missing behaviour, the minimum code that passes, then a refactor that keeps the assertion. This document adds none of that code.
